**Fork 1 v1.0.1** — patch release: two cosmetic fixes to the v1.0.0 theme variants, both found by the core-UI simulator's pre-flash renders and verified fixed by re-rendering from the patched sources (fork commit 90a452d). No feature change, no ABI change.

## What changed

- **Separators:** the standby renderers used `·` middle dots in 5 places (Clean Modern info line / card title / lock hint, Data Dense status line). The radio's embedded-graphics fonts have no middle-dot glyph, so on hardware they would have printed as `?`. They are now ASCII `|` — e.g. `GMRS 20 | MAIN`, `Simplex | No tone | High`, `WATCH B | DW ON | PS OFF`. Verified: no `?` in any simulator frame, and no non-ASCII string literals remain anywhere in the fork's `src/`.
- **Clean Modern palette:** v1.0.0 shipped a saturated royal blue (dominant panel colour (24,48,156)); the approved mockup is a muted desaturated slate. The palette was retuned from pixel samples of the mockup: dominant panel **(24,48,156) → (24,44,57)** (mockup sample (23,44,60)), background (8,24,82) → (16,24,33) (mockup (16,24,32)), dim text → (140,166,181) (mockup (140,165,185)). The accent already matched the mockup and is unchanged, as are the semantic colours and all geometry; the other three themes' palettes are untouched.
